## How to read the skin's console

`skin stats` prints one line: `skin on frames=F words=W draw_us=D peak_us=P dropped=X ballast=N`.

- `words` is the last skinned frame's display-list length, the ballast included.
- `draw_us` is that frame's draw time on the CPU, and `peak_us` the longest draw in the last whole second.
- `dropped` counts the shapes and texts the frame's queue lost. It must read 0 on every skinned screen: a lost label shows here and
  nowhere else.
- `skin on (no bitmaps: classic drawn)` means the bitmaps did not upload at boot. Stop and report: nothing in this page applies.

`skin ballast N` adds N words after the frame (N/2 one-pixel rectangles at (0, 479), the fill-rate app's method; clamped at 1,900) and
answers with the same line. `skin ballast 0` takes it away. `skin on` and `skin off` also answer with it. `skin stats` counts only the
skinned screens: LIBRARY, Config and HELP keep the kit's drawing and do not count.

Screens by button: Pattern is HOME (a tap on HOME opens PATTERN SETUP), Params is INSTRUMENT (again: the next page), Performance is
PERFORM (again: PERFORM SETUP), Song is SONG, Master is MASTER, File is FILE PROJECTS (again: LIBRARY), Sample Rec is RECORD, the other
Sample buttons are HOOKS, Shift + Config is HELP. The simulator's pictures of every skinned screen are in `docs/manual/img/` and come from
`app/scenarios/skin_*.txt`: run one with `build-host/app/o3_303_sim --scenario app/scenarios/skin_home.txt --out <dir>` to compare a
`--shot` with its frame.

## After the session

- The ceiling: `min(1,600, L - 200)` with the lowest L of A5, B2 and B4 goes into `tests/o3_303_scenarios_test.cpp` (SKIN-PLAN 4.3).
  Under 1,550, HOME sheds words first (4.6: the sheen, the texture as one band, a shorter motion lane), and a screen that cannot shed
  enough falls back to classic.
- A3 or A4 fails (a broken line below the ceiling): the per-line budget of 4.4 drops to 80 % of the failing frame's line load, and the
  frame is trimmed the same way.
- SU2 passed (Part B with no fail) opens SK8, OBSIDIAN by default.
