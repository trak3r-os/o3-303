Prepared, not run. The image carries eight items of `docs/BACKLOG.md`: 11, 4, 12 and 17 (B1), then 13, 1, 7 and 6 (B2). This page checks what
a host test cannot: the real pads, LEDs and display, and the render budget with the new code.

Rows marked (eyes) need the maintainer at the unit, and rows marked (ears) need them listening. The tester leaves those rows open and
goes on. Every other row runs by the console: `key`, `enc`, `state`, `leds`, `cpu`, `crash`, `log`, `version`, `files`, `reset`.

The unit is shared. Ask for it through Comunicación con agentes, as the session's orchestrator does, and say "libre" there when done. Note
the start state and restore exactly it. A source is connected to the line-in: leave it connected. An install loses the unsaved STATUS LED
setting of Config > DISPLAY; the maintainer agreed to that for tonight's rounds. Edits happen on `UNTITLED` with AUTOSAVE OFF only, and a
`reset` drops them. No RECORD take: a take writes to `/Samples/`, and neither the app nor the console can delete it.
