## Reading

The limit is the number of sprites on a line (about 216 at cell 100, about 224 at cell 50 for F1), not the pixel count. Bilinear scaling
costs about four times as much as plain sprites.

The skin's busiest line is 4,208 px (2,034 bitmap plus 2,174 fill). MIX at cell 50 was clean at 6,400 px, and 75 % of that is 4,800 px,
which is at least 4,208. So the skin fits within the README's margin at the same cell width, with ballast 0.

Not shown: the margin at the skin's real display-list length. At ballast 768 only K16 at cell 800 was tried, and it flickered.

For the skin:
- no BILINEAR;
- keep the sprite count per line low (merge small cells);
- measure the real skin frame on the unit before relying on it.
