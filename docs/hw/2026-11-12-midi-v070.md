# Unit session 2026-11-12: USB MIDI on v0.7.0 with tools/midi_check.py

Run by the session o3-303-ad, about 45 minutes: the tool's first run on a unit (`docs/hw/2026-11-01-midi.md`, rows 1 to 7). Phases identity,
notes, follow (long run 300 s) and clockout; learn, stress and soak were not run. The computer was building at the same time (load average
about 5), so its clock sender and reader were late at times: read the worst-case rows with that in mind. DIN is still owed (no interface here).

What held, with numbers: every note counted (10 of 10, 400 of 400 in the burst, dropped 0, no stuck note); lock in 20 to 24 clocks from 60
to 300 BPM with a tempo error of 0.004 BPM mean and 0.03 worst; tempo steps reacquired in 0.41 to 0.72 s; a dropout of 12 clocks held, release
after 48 clocks, Stop in 11 ms, Continue in 35 ms, SPP taken; the long run's phase offset 0.38 ms median, 0.35 ms rms, drift -0.02 ms in 300 s;
clock out within 0.0024 % with 0.39 to 0.52 ms rms (the 1 ms USB frame gives 0.41). Render max 56.4 %, 0 underruns over 740,770 blocks.

The failures, read one by one:

- **Pitch and CC 123: the tool on this patch, not the firmware.** By hand (`amidi`, `arecord`), a held note 43 reads 49.00 Hz, a G to the
  cent, and CC 120 and CC 123 give the same decay to the half second (-42, -55, -67 dBFS at 0.5 s steps: the delay's tail). The tool's audio
  limits need the default patch; owed is a tool run on one.
- **`locked` flicker (3 readouts of 238, and under +-2 ms jitter)**: the known lock band (5 %), the tempo held throughout. Cosmetic.
- **Long run: +2 clocks out against 14,400 sent, one offset of 18.3 ms.** Not explained. The sender's worst lateness was 3.1 ms. Owed: the
  same run on an idle computer, and MC1's cold-start figure (first clock out 4 to 6 ms after the first clock in), which the tool does not print.
- **Clock out's worst interval** moved between runs (6.9, 3.2, 2.3 ms) with no missing or extra clock: more likely the reader than the unit.
  Owed: the same on an idle computer.
- **"Tempo set exactly"**: the project's tempo is 115.606, so the knob lands on x.106; the tool measured against the unit's own readout.
