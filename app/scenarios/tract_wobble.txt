# The wobble on the tract's MOUTH (docs/TRACT-KNOBS-PLAN.md A2, 11.5): WOBBLE's TARGET has a third word, MOUTH, after CUTOFF and VOWEL: the sequencer's wobble swings the TRACT's VOWEL around
# the vowel that sounds, as it swings the growl's. With the tract off the key refuses it ("TRACT IS OFF" for a second, the target stays on VOWEL), as VOWEL with the growl off says GROWL
# IS OFF. TRACT: the vowel I (pad), the mix at 100 %; WOBBLE: RATE 1/8, DEPTH 100 %, TARGET MOUTH, then the latch (F8) on and the factory line plays: the WAV shows the vowel moving at 1/8.
#
#   mkdir -p <dir> && build-host/app/o3_303_sim --scenario app/scenarios/tract_wobble.txt --out <dir>
#
# 01 WOBBLE with the growl on and the tract off: the TARGET turned to VOWEL, a turn on to MOUTH is refused (the toast on HOME); 02 the tract on; 03 TARGET MOUTH; 04 the latch on; 05 the line plays.
wait 300
record on
# TRACT: on, mix 100 %, the vowel I by its pad. The growl is on too (F8 of GROWL) so VOWEL is a target one step before MOUTH.
tap sampleedit   # TRACT (Sample Editor, E1a)
tap f8
tap f7
turn 100
paddown 38
wait 60
padup 38
tap sampleplay
tap f8
wait 50
snap 00_growl_on
tap samplerec   # WOBBLE (Sample Recorder, E1a)
tap f3
turn 50
tap f4
turn 1
wait 100
snap 01_target_vowel
tap f4
turn 1
wait 100
snap 02_target_mouth
tap f8
wait 100
snap 03_wobble_latched
tap play
wait 3400
snap 04_wobble_on_mouth
tap play
wait 300
wav tract_wobble
