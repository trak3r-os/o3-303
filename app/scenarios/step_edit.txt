# Step editing of M3b (docs/M3B-PLAN.md R3 layer rotate, R4 ratchets and the step's octave, R5 trig conditions) on the factory line in A01.
# The step view does not draw ratchets and conditions yet (the step-view round does); the evidence here is the info strip's line (STEP n RATCHET xn,
# STEP n COND ...), the PERF page's strip and the note and accent rows moving. Step pads: steps 1..8 are pads 0..7, steps 9..16 pads 12..19.
wait 300
# Hold step 4 and tap OCT+ (pad 11) twice: its ratchet is x3. OCT- (pad 10) takes one back. The keyboard's octave does not move.
paddown 3
pad 11
pad 11
wait 100
snap 01_ratchet_x3
pad 10
wait 100
snap 02_ratchet_x2
padup 3
# Hold step 6 and tap the up arrow: that step's note moves an octave (up / down alone are one semitone on the cursor step).
paddown 5
tap up
wait 100
snap 03_step_octave_up
padup 5
tap up
wait 100
snap 04_cursor_semitone
# The PERF page (Shift + Params): F7 is RATCHET and F8 is COND for the step held, or for the cursor step when none is.
press shift
tap params
release shift
tap f7
paddown 7
turn 2
wait 100
snap 05_perf_ratchet_encoder
padup 7
tap f8
paddown 7
turn 10
wait 100
snap 06_cond_one_of_two
padup 7

turn 45
wait 100
snap 07_cond_fill
padup 15

press shift
turn 1
release shift
wait 100
snap 08_cond_coarse
padup 15
tap params
# Hold ACC (pad 8): the step pads show the steps that have the accent; right rotates only the accents one step later.

wait 100

tap right
wait 100
