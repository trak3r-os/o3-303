# The scale-locked keyboard (docs/M3B-PLAN.md R8): with a scale chosen in the RANDOM popup (Shift + F1, F1 is Scale) the keyboard pads out of it are
# dark and do nothing; a boot has none chosen (the generator's default scale is not a choice), so the keyboard is chromatic until the Scale key is
# turned. The popup sets it through the same control as always, so the lock engages as before.
wait 300
snap 01_chromatic_boot
# The popup: F1 is Scale; walk to the first entry (No scale) and up to Major (3).
press shift
tap f1
release shift
tap f1
turn -40
turn 3
wait 100
snap 02_major_chosen
press shift
tap f1
release shift
wait 100
snap 03_c_major_keyboard
# REC + keys: D (pad 38) and E (pad 40) are written on steps 1 and 2; the C# (37) and D# (39) pads, dark, do nothing and the cursor stays.
tap rec
pad 37
pad 38
pad 39
pad 40
wait 100
snap 04_rec_in_scale_only
tap rec
# C minor pentatonic: Penta Min is entry 18.
press shift
tap f1
release shift
tap f1
turn -40
turn 18
press shift
tap f1
release shift
wait 100
snap 05_penta_minor
# No scale (0) unlocks it again.
press shift
tap f1
release shift
tap f1
turn -40
press shift
tap f1
release shift
wait 100
snap 06_no_scale
