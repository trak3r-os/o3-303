# The scale-locked keyboard (docs/M3B-PLAN.md R8): with a scale chosen on PATTERN SETUP (a tap of Pattern; F5 is Scale) the keyboard pads out of it are
# dark and do nothing; a boot has none chosen (the generator's default scale is not a choice), so the keyboard is chromatic until the Scale key is
# turned. SETUP sets it through the same control as always, so the lock engages as before.
wait 300
snap 01_chromatic_boot
# SETUP: F5 is Scale; walk to the first entry (No scale) and up to Major (3).
tap pattern
tap f5
turn -40
turn 3
wait 100
snap 02_major_chosen
tap pattern
wait 100
snap 03_c_major_keyboard
# REC + keys: D (pad 38) and E (pad 40) are written on steps 1 and 2; the C# (37) and D# (39) pads, dark, do nothing and the cursor stays.
tap rec
pad 37
pad 38
pad 39
pad 40
wait 100
snap 04_rec_in_scale_only
tap rec
# C minor pentatonic: Penta Min is entry 18.
tap pattern
tap f5
turn -40
turn 18
tap pattern
wait 100
snap 05_penta_minor
# No scale (0) unlocks it again.
tap pattern
tap f5
turn -40
tap pattern
wait 100
snap 06_no_scale
