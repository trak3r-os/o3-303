# The four performance buttons (docs/M3B-PLAN.md C4): Note, Instr, Vol and Fx are MUTE, ACC ALL, FILL and FREEZE by default, and each takes one of
# MUTE, ACC ALL, SLIDE ALL, OCT UP, FILL, THROW, FREEZE on the PERF page (three Params from SOUND; F1..F4 are the buttons, the
# encoder walks the actions). Held is momentary, Shift latches. The header's chips say what each button is now.
wait 300
tap params
tap params
tap params
wait 100
snap 01_perf_page_defaults
# Note becomes OCT UP: the running pattern goes up an octave while it is held, on top of the keyboard's own transposition.
tap f1
turn 3
wait 100
snap 02_note_is_oct_up
tap play
wait 700
press note
wait 400
snap 03_oct_up_held
release note
wait 400
# Instr becomes THROW and Vol SLIDE ALL (two the defaults left); Shift + the button latches.
tap f2
turn -8
turn 5
tap f3
turn -8
turn 2
wait 100
snap 04_perf_page_reassigned
press shift
tap instr
tap vol
release shift
wait 500
snap 05_throw_and_slide_latched
press shift
tap instr
tap vol
release shift
# FILL (back on Vol) with its variant OCT UP, held; Fx is FREEZE: held, the chip lights.
tap f3
turn -8
turn 4
# on to the SEQ page (SNAP, SOUND, FX, SEQ): F6 is what FILL plays
tap params
tap params
tap params
tap params
tap f6
turn 1
press vol
wait 700
snap 06_fill_oct_up
press fx
wait 400
snap 07_fill_and_freeze_held
release fx
release vol
tap play
