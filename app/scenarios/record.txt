# RECORD (docs/UX-303.md 5.10, D14): bar-aligned takes of the master into the 30 s take buffer, saved to Samples/take_NNN.wav.
#
#   mkdir <dir> <card> && build-host/app/o3_303_sim --scenario app/scenarios/record.txt --card <card> --out <dir>
#
# Start from an empty card directory: it ends up with Samples/take_001.wav (a 4 bar take, saved by F6) and take_002.wav (1 bar, Shift + Rec). Without --card (the scenarios test runs it so)
# the same keys say NO CARD and the takes stay until F7 drops them; the pictures are the same but for the card line. The simulator has no codec input unless it is given one
# (--audio-in), so F2 says NO AUDIO INPUT here: the input sources are the unit's and the audio-in scenarios'.
# The pictures: 01 idle, 03 and 04 the tempo too slow for 8 bars, 06 armed (waiting for the bar), 07 recording, 08 done, 10 saved, 12 Shift + Rec armed on HOME, 13 the take on HOME, 19 and 20 the take refused on PERFORM and on HOOKS.
record on
wait 300
tap samplerec
wait 100
snap 01_idle
# F2 SOURCE: there is no input to go to.
tap f2
wait 50
snap 02_no_input
# F3 BARS goes round 4, 8, FREE, 1, 2. The tempo drops to 55 BPM (MASTER, TEMPO on the encoder: half a BPM a detent): 8 bars no longer fit 30 s and F1 refuses them on the screen.
tap f3
wait 50
tap master
turn -70
turn -70
tap samplerec
wait 100
snap 03_bars_too_long
tap f1
wait 100
snap 04_refused
# FREE, 1, 2 and 4 bars: 4 bars fit at 55 BPM (17.5 s). Back to 125 BPM, the line plays, F1 arms a 4 bar take and it starts at the next bar.
tap f3
tap f3
tap f3
tap f3
tap master
turn 70
turn 70
tap samplerec
wait 100
snap 05_four_bars
tap play
wait 700
tap f1
wait 100
snap 06_armed
wait 2000
snap 07_recording
wait 7600

# F6 saves it to Samples/ (with a card; without one it says NO CARD).
tap f6

# F7 DISCARD asks for a second press within 5 s; the second drops the take (nothing to drop after a save: NO TAKE).
tap f7
wait 100

tap pattern
