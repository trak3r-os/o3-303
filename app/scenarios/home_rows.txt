# The drawer rows (docs/UX-303.md 2.3 and 5.1, slice H3): Note, Instr, Vol and Fx latch the NOTE, GATE, PLAYS and LOCK rows of the drawer on the encoder
# (the ENC text and the highlighted caption say which), an F key gives it back to the knob, and Shift + a colour button is the button alone. The rows edit
# the held steps, else the cursor's: NOTE a semitone (Shift an octave), GATE the list REST NOTE ACC SLD ACC+SLD TIE, PLAYS the list x1..x4, 75 / 50 / 25 %,
# 1:2 .. 4:4, FILL, !FILL, FIRST, !FIRST (a ratchet or a condition, one at a time), LOCK the lock of the latched knob (below its minimum: "--", no lock).
# The STEP box on the info line shows the selected step whole. The live actions of these buttons are PERFORM's (perform.txt). Step pads: steps 1..8 are
