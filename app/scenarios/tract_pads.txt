# The pads of the TRACT page (docs/PADS-TRACT-PLAN.md, the redesign of 2026-11-13). 0-based, row 0 at the top: rows 0 and 1 columns 0 to 7 are the 16 steps; column 9 holds the two RANDOMIZEs
# (row 0: the whole tract pattern, vowels plus TONGUE and LIPS locks; row 1: the vowels only), column 11 PLAY TRACT (row 0: the pattern or the knobs drive the mouth; the LED blinks high playing, low
# stopped) and VOWEL MODE (row 1). Columns 8 and 10 are dark. Normal mode, row 2: A E I, TONGUE up (column 4), LIPS up (column 6); row 3: O U "-", TONGUE down, LIPS down. VOWEL MODE, row 2: A E I O U
