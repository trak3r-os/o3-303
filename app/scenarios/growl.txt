# The growl and the wobble (docs/M3C-PLAN.md G and W): GROWL (Sample Playback) opens the growl's Mix and sets a vowel and WOBBLE (Sample Editor) points the
# wobble at the growl's Vowel (rate 1/8, depth 100 %), PERFORM SETUP gives Fx the WOBBLE action, and the factory line plays. Held, the WOBBLE action
# runs the vowel at 1/8 (A -> U -> A about the middle vowel, I, twice a beat: the talking bass); the release gives the pattern's rate back (the wobble
# is off). The rate picker is PERFORM's row 3 (perform_pads.txt), not HOME's keys. The WAV is the evidence: it is the 303
# through the formants and the comb, dry for the first bar, wobbling for the next two, plain again at the end.
wait 300
record on
# GROWL (Sample Playback twice, TRACT then GROWL, 91 ms each: the 186 ms the one tap of 184 took, which were the three Params taps of the old INSTRUMENT MODS, so every later event keeps its time and the WAV its bytes): F1 VOWEL to 50 % (I),
# F7 GRL MIX to 100 %; WOBBLE (Sample Editor): F3 DEPTH to 100 %, F4 TARGET the growl's Vowel. The wait is what is left of the old 100 ms and the one tap more.
tap sampleplay 91   # TRACT first, then GROWL: the two taps take the 186 ms the one did
tap sampleplay 91
tap f1
turn 50
tap f7
turn 100
snap 01a_growl_set
tap sampleedit
tap f3
turn 50
tap f4
turn 1
wait 38
snap 01_growl_and_wobble_set
# PERFORM SETUP (Shift + Perform): Fx (F4) gets the WOBBLE action (the eleven actions: down to the first, then up seven); Perform comes PERFORM back, where the buttons are the live actions.
press shift
tap performance
release shift
tap f4
turn -16
turn 7
wait 100
snap 02_perf_wobble_on_fx
tap performance
wait 162
tap play
wait 2100
# Fx held: the vowel wobbles at the set 1/8.
press fx
wait 2400
snap 04_wobble_held
# The wobble goes on at its set 1/8 (PERFORM's row 3 is the picker, and none is picked here), for as long as the picker used to take.
wait 1802
snap 05_wobble_goes_on
wait 1802
release fx
wait 1500
snap 06_wobble_released
tap play
wait 600
wav growl_wobble
