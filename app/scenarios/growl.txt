# The growl and the wobble (docs/M3C-PLAN.md G and W): the GROWL page (after FX) opens the growl's Mix and sets a vowel, the WOBBLE page points the
# wobble at the growl's Vowel (rate 1/8, depth 100 %), the PERF page gives Fx the WOBBLE action, and the factory line plays. Held, the WOBBLE action
# runs the vowel at 1/8 (A -> U -> A about the middle vowel, I, twice a beat: the talking bass); the release gives the pattern's rate back (the wobble
# is off). The rate picker is PERFORM's row 3 (perform_pads.txt), not HOME's keys. The WAV is the evidence: it is the 303
# through the formants and the comb, dry for the first bar, wobbling for the next two, plain again at the end.
wait 300
record on
# GROWL (two Params from SOUND): F1 VOWEL to 50 % (I), F7 GROWL MIX to 100 %.
tap params
tap params
tap f1
turn 50
tap f7
turn 100
wait 100
snap 01_growl_page
# WOBBLE: F3 DEPTH 100 %, F5 TARGET the growl's Vowel.
tap params
tap f3
turn 50
tap f5
turn 1
wait 100
snap 02_wobble_page
# PERF (two more Params): Fx (F4) gets the WOBBLE action (the eleven actions: down to the first, then up seven); two more Params come back.
tap params
tap params
tap f4
turn -16
turn 7
wait 100
snap 03_perf_wobble_on_fx
tap params
tap params
tap play
wait 2100
# Fx held: the vowel wobbles at the set 1/8.
press fx
wait 2400
snap 04_wobble_held
# The wobble goes on at its set 1/8 (HOME's pads are keys: no rate is picked here), for as long as the picker used to take.
wait 1802
snap 05_wobble_goes_on
wait 1802
release fx
wait 1500
snap 06_wobble_released
tap play
wait 600
wav growl_wobble
