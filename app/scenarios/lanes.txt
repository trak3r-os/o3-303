# Motion lanes through the pattern's lane table (docs/M3B-PLAN.md C2 and the review's finding 4): a lane drives the parameter its LaneRef says, so
# any key of the FX page can have one. Hold MOTION (pad 23) + an F key points the lane the screen shows (the last SOUND key's) at that key's
# parameter; a p-lock (hold a step + the encoder) and a recorded move then go to that lane, scaled with the target's own descriptor.
wait 300
# SOUND: F4 DECAY shows lane 4 (DECAY) under the step view.
tap f4
wait 100
snap 01_decay_lane
# FX page, hold MOTION and tap F1 DRIVE: the lane that showed DECAY now drives the distortion's Drive.
tap params
paddown 23
tap f1
wait 100
snap 02_lane_repointed_to_drive
padup 23
# Hold step 3 and turn DRIVE: a lock on that lane, scaled to the distortion's own range (the strip shows it as LOCK 3).
paddown 2
turn -12
wait 100
snap 03_drive_plock
padup 2
paddown 6
turn -30
wait 100
padup 6
wait 100
snap 04_drive_locks
# VOLUME (SOUND F8) is the mixer's level, lane 8: a lock there is in the mixer's own 0..1.5 range.
tap params
tap params
tap params
tap f8
paddown 4
turn 8
wait 100
snap 05_volume_plock
padup 4
# The old lane is gone: DECAY has no lane now, so hold a step + the encoder on it moves the knob and says so.
tap f4
paddown 13
turn 5
wait 100
snap 06_decay_has_no_lane
padup 13
# Undo (Shift + F8) brings back the lane's old target.
press shift
tap f8
release shift
press shift
tap f8
release shift
press shift
tap f8
release shift
press shift
tap f8
release shift
wait 100
snap 07_undone
