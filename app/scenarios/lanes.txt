# Motion lanes through the pattern's lane table (docs/M3B-PLAN.md C2, UX-303 D2): a lane drives the parameter its LaneRef says, so any key of the FX page
# can have one; the first lock on a parameter no lane drives claims the first lane with no motion, in the same undoable edit. A p-lock (hold a step +
# the encoder) and a recorded move go to that lane, scaled with the target's own descriptor.
wait 300
# SOUND: F4 DECAY shows lane 4 (DECAY) under the step view.
tap f4
wait 100
snap 01_decay_lane
# FX page, F1 DRIVE: no lane drives it. Hold step 3 and turn it: the lock claims a lane (the first with no motion, lane 1: the cutoff's, which had no
# locks) and the strip shows it as LOCK 3, scaled to the distortion's own range.
tap params
tap f1
paddown 2
turn -12
wait 100
snap 02_drive_lock_claims_a_lane
padup 2
paddown 6
turn -30
wait 100
padup 6
wait 100
snap 03_drive_locks
