# The TRACT line on the TRACT page's LINE layer (docs/TRACT-KNOBS-PLAN.md A2 rest; the page opens on STEPS since E2a, a second Sample Editor press is LINE): the pads are the pattern's line. Rows 1 and 2 (pads 0 to 23) are its steps, 24 a page; row 3 is the palette
# (pads 24 to 28 write A E I O U, 29 writes "-", 34 is GLD, 35 is LINE); row 4 (pads 36 to 47) stays live: with LINE off a tap writes VOWEL, with LINE on and the pattern playing a hold
# overrides the line and the release gives it back. A write goes to the held steps, else to the cursor, and the cursor moves on. Shift + F4 is LEN, Shift + F5 CLEAR.
#
#   mkdir -p <dir> && build-line-host/app/o3_303_sim --scenario app/scenarios/tract_line.txt --out <dir>
#
# 01 TRACT, stopped, the line empty; 02 a line typed over the factory pattern: A E I O U - A E (the cursor on step 8); 03 GLD on the step 7 (E glides in); 04 LEN 8: the steps past it go dark;
# 05 playing with LINE on: the playhead runs over the line; 06 a live vowel held on row 4: the line stands aside; 07 released: the line takes over at the next step; 08 LINE off, playing:
# the line is stored but the vowel is the knob's; 09 CLEAR. The WAV: the factory line under the tract, the line moving the vowel for two bars, a hold on O, the line again, LINE off.
wait 300
record on
tap sampleedit   # TRACT (Sample Editor, E1a), the STEPS layer
tap sampleedit   # again: the LINE layer (E2a), where the pads are the line
wait 100
tap f8
tap f7
turn 50
wait 100
snap 01_tract_stopped
# Typing the line: palette pads write at the cursor, which moves on.
pad 24 40
pad 25 40
pad 26 40
pad 27 40
pad 28 40
pad 29 40
pad 24 40
pad 25 40
wait 100
snap 02_line_typed
# GLD on step 7 (select it, then the GLD pad).
pad 7 40
pad 34 40
wait 100
snap 03_gld_on_step_7
# LEN: Shift + F4 twice, SAME to 4 to 8.
press shift
tap f4
tap f4
release shift
wait 100
snap 04_len_8
# LINE on, play: the playhead runs over the line.
pad 35 40
tap play
wait 2400
snap 05_playing_line_on
# A live vowel held on row 4 (pad 39 is O): the line stands aside.
paddown 39
wait 900
snap 06_live_hold
padup 39
wait 1500
snap 07_released
# LINE off: the line is stored, the vowel is the knob's.
pad 35 40
wait 1600
snap 08_line_off_playing
tap play
wait 300
press shift
tap f5
release shift
wait 100
snap 09_cleared
wait 300
wav tract_line
