# GROWL and WOBBLE, the two screens of the Sample Playback and Sample Editor buttons (docs/MODS-PLAN.md 5.1, 5.4, 5.5; S0): InstrumentScreen with a one-page group each.
# GROWL: seven knobs, the formant (F1 VOWEL, F2 FMT Q, F3 FMT MIX), the comb (F4 CMB TUNE, F5 CMB FB, F6 CMB MIX) and the growl's mix (F7 GRL MIX), F8 an empty well; the sections
# VOICE and WOBBLE under the knobs. WOBBLE: F1 RATE, F2 SHAPE, F3 DEPTH, F4 TARGET, F5 RESET (the wobble's settings are app values), F6 to F8 empty wells; the sections VOICE and GROWL.
# Both keep the home map on the pads and the drawer under the knobs, so the line keeps playing and editing while the sound is set. The Shift row is MIDI learn: LEARN on every
# growl knob (they are unit parameters); on WOBBLE (app values) there is no row and the hint says NOT LEARNABLE (U8a). A held step + a turn locks the latched growl knob on that step.
#
#   mkdir -p <dir> && build-host/app/o3_303_sim --scenario app/scenarios/growl_wobble.txt --out <dir> --midi
#
# 01 GROWL (classic): the seven knobs, F1 latched; 02 a lock of F6 CMB MIX on a held step; 03 Shift: LEARN on each knob; 04 Shift + F6 arms it; 05 the controller binds it; 06 WOBBLE
# (classic): the five keys and the three empty wells; 07 Shift: no LEARN row, the hint NOT LEARNABLE, F1 cannot be armed; 08 Sample Playback again from WOBBLE; 09 Params from GROWL opens VOICE.
