# The TRACT page's STEPS layer (docs/PADS-TRACT-PLAN.md E2a): the page opens on the pattern's steps, rows 1 and 2 columns 1 to 8 (pads 0 to 7 and 12 to 19; the other four of each row do nothing).
# A tap selects a step; the palette on row 3 (pads 24 to 28 write A E I O U, 29 writes "-", 34 is GLD, 35 is LINE) writes the TRACT line under the held steps, else under the cursor, which moves
# on. A held step with F1 turned moves that step's line vowel, with F2 or F3 turned it locks TONGUE or LIPS for that step. The line has its own length: LEN 4 puts the pattern's steps 4 to 7 on the
# line's 0 to 3 and the toast says the edit repeats. Sample Editor again is the LINE layer (tract_line.txt), and again STEPS.
#
#   mkdir -p <dir> && build-host/app/o3_303_sim --scenario app/scenarios/tract_steps.txt --out <dir>
#

# moves from I to O; 05 step 3 held and F2 turned: a TONGUE lock; 06 the LINE layer (a second press); 07 STEPS again; 08 LEN 4 and an edit on step 6: the toast. The WAV: the line playing with LINE on.
