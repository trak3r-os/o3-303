# The pad filter ladders (docs/UX-303.md 6.2): with FILT on (pad 23, a toggle at the press) rows 3 and 4 of the pads are 12 places of Cutoff and Reso; a
# tap jumps there through a 30 ms slew; the LEDs draw where the knob stands as a bar; FILT off gives the keyboard back. Row 3 starts at pad 24, row 4 at
# pad 36.
wait 300
paddown 23
padup 23
wait 99
snap 01_filt_on_default
# Cutoff to the sixth place (pad 29), Reso to the ninth (pad 44).
pad 29
pad 44
wait 100

# The strip shows the knobs where the ladder put them (F1 and F2).
pad 24
pad 47
wait 100
