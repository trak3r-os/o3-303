# MASTER (docs/UX-303.md 5.6): the tempo large, the nudge, the clock, the master's peaks as two meters; F1 TEMPO latched on entry, F2 TAP, F3 NUDGE (hold + turn),
# F4 MASTER; the pads are HOME's; the colour buttons do nothing here. Choices made here: CPU % is left off (D19: the kit's header strip already shows it); the meters
# are on a -48 dB .. 0 dB scale with a peak tick that falls slowly.
wait 300
tap master
wait 100
snap 01_opened_stopped
# The encoder is on TEMPO: turn.
turn 10
wait 100
snap 02_tempo_up
# TAP: four taps 500 ms apart make 120 BPM; the box counts them and the tick shows in the readout.
tap f2 30
wait 470
tap f2 30
wait 470
tap f2 30
wait 470
tap f2 30
wait 200
snap 03_tapped_120
# F3 NUDGE: hold + turn; the readout is amber while it is not zero, the box F3 shows it.
press f3
turn 3
wait 200
snap 04_nudge_f3_held
release f3
wait 100
# F4 MASTER takes the encoder: the level.
tap f4
turn -6
wait 100
snap 05_master_level
# Play: the meters move with the factory line.
tap f1
tap play
wait 1500
snap 06_playing_meters
wait 400
snap 07_playing_meters_later
tap play
wait 2500
snap 08_stopped_peaks_falling
# The pads are HOME's: a step pad toggles its step, the PADS line is HOME's.
paddown 30
wait 50
snap 09_a_pad_held
padup 30
# Master pressed again on MASTER puts the encoder back on TEMPO; the colour buttons do nothing.
tap master
press note
wait 100
snap 10_colour_button_does_nothing
release note
