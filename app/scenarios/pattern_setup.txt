# one gesture is one UNDO; F2 SWING, F3 GATE, F4 TRANSP, F5 CHANCE is the share of the steps that play; the Shift row has CLR LOCK, COPY PAT, PASTE, DUP>NEXT, REVERSE, DOUBLE, REDO and UNDO. The generator's knobs are on GENERATOR (generator.txt).
