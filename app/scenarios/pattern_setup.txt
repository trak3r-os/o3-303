# PATTERN SETUP (docs/UX-303.md 5.2): a tap of Pattern on HOME opens it, a tap again goes back. F1 LENGTH up copies the line into the new half and down truncates,
# each one UNDO; F5 SCALE locks the keyboard; F6..F8 are the generator's settings (project-wide); the Shift row has COPY PAT, PASTE PAT, DUP>NEXT, CLEAR LOCKS and UNDO.
wait 300
tap pattern
wait 100
snap 01_setup_open
# F1 LENGTH is latched: one detent up is 32, the new half is the line again.
turn 1
wait 100
snap 02_length_32
# Two down is 8: the steps past the 8th are cut. One UNDO gives the 32 back, a second the 16 the line started with.
turn -2
wait 100
snap 03_length_8
press shift
tap f8
release shift
wait 100
snap 04_undo_to_32
press shift
tap f8
release shift
wait 100
snap 05_undo_to_16
# F5 SCALE: walk to Major (entry 3); the keyboard pads out of it go dark and the info strip says so.
tap f5
turn -40
turn 3
wait 100
snap 06_scale_major
# The generator's other three: DENSITY down, ACC % up, SLD % down.
tap f6
turn -3
tap f7
turn 2
tap f8
turn -2
wait 100
snap 07_generator
# A lock on step 3 (HOME's gesture works on SETUP too: the knob is a SETUP one, so only HOME's F1 CUTOFF locks): back on HOME, hold step 3 and turn.
tap pattern
paddown 2
turn 20
padup 2
paddown 6
turn -30
padup 6
wait 100
snap 08_home_with_locks
tap pattern
wait 100
snap 09_setup_with_locks
# The Shift row, then CLEAR LOCKS: every lane of this pattern, the notes stay.
press shift
wait 700
snap 10_shift_row
tap f4
release shift
wait 100
snap 11_locks_cleared
# COPY PAT, then DUP>NEXT: A01 is copied into A02, which is selected; PASTE PAT puts the clipboard over A02.
press shift
tap f1
release shift
press shift
tap f3
release shift
wait 100
snap 12_dup_to_next
press shift
tap f2
release shift
wait 100
snap 13_pasted
# Pattern held on SETUP is the 48 patterns, as on HOME; its release does not flip the page.
press pattern
wait 350
snap 14_grid
release pattern
wait 100
snap 15_still_setup
tap pattern
wait 100
snap 16_back_on_home
# HOME with the Major lock still on, a pattern queued and a step held with a lock: the strip is full, so the scale takes its short form ("SC MAJ").
tap play
wait 200
press pattern
wait 350
pad 3
release pattern
paddown 4
turn 30
wait 100
snap 17_home_scale_short
padup 4
tap play
