# O3-303 booting: the 303 screen with the vault's first factory line (bank A, slot 0) in pattern A01, a key on each keyboard row sounding
# the voice, the keyboard an octave up (OCT+ pad), then Play: the playhead runs, the WAV is the voice and its sequencer.
# The tempo, the PLAY / STOP badge and (on the unit) the CPU % are the kit's header strip at the top; the strip under it is the 303's own
# (pattern, LEN, SW, GATE, REC, KEYS / TRNSP, NEXT, and the four performance chips), so nothing is shown twice.
wait 300
snap 01_boot
record on
paddown 36
wait 250
snap 02_key
padup 36
wait 150
paddown 24
wait 250
padup 24
wait 150
pad 11
paddown 36
wait 250
snap 03_octave_up
padup 36
wait 200
tap play
wait 1300
snap 04_playing
tap play
wait 500
