# The TRACT page (docs/TRACT-KNOBS-PLAN.md A2, 3.3, 11.1): Sample Playback opens TRACT (it used to open GROWL; again it flips to GROWL and back), F1 VOWEL, F2 TONGUE, F3 LIPS, F4 RES,
# F5 GLIDE, F6 SIZE, F7 TRC MIX, F8 the TRACT key (off is Mix 0, a bit-exact bypass; on gives the last mix back). The bottom row of pads holds the live vowels A E I O U: a tap writes
# VOWEL, the LED of the vowel that sounds is on and the other four dim; the other pads (rows 1 to 3 are the TRACT pattern's, later) are dark and do nothing. VOWEL reads a letter on a
# vowel's point ("A", "O") and "<nearest letter> <percent>" between ("E 37%").
#
#   mkdir -p <dir> && build-host/app/o3_303_sim --scenario app/scenarios/tract.txt --out <dir>
#
# 01 TRACT at a first press (F8 off, the vowel pads lit); 02 F8 on (Mix 50 %); 03 the vowel O by its pad; 04 the mouth in between (F1 turned to 37 %: "E 37%"); 05 a pad outside the
# vowels pressed: nothing changes; 06 Sample Playback again: GROWL; 07 again: TRACT back with its vowel; 08 F8 off. The WAV is the factory line: dry for the first bar, the tract on a
# moving vowel for two, dry again at the end (the bit-exact A/B of the bypass is tests/tract_page_test.cpp).
wait 300
record on
tap play
wait 1900
tap sampleedit   # TRACT (Sample Editor, E1a)
wait 100
snap 01_tract_first_press
tap f8
wait 100
snap 02_tract_on
# The knobs: F7 TRC MIX to 100 % and the mouth narrowed (F2 TONGUE, F3 LIPS), then the vowels by their pads.
tap f7
turn 50
tap f2
turn 30
tap f3
turn -20
paddown 39
wait 60
padup 39
wait 600
snap 03_vowel_o_by_pad
paddown 36
wait 60
padup 36
wait 700
paddown 37
wait 60
padup 37
wait 700
tap f1
turn 12
wait 100
snap 04_vowel_between
paddown 3
wait 60
padup 3
paddown 41
wait 60
padup 41
wait 800
snap 05_other_pads_do_nothing
paddown 40
wait 60
padup 40
wait 900
tap sampleplay
wait 100
snap 06_growl_by_the_second_press
tap sampleedit   # TRACT (Sample Editor, E1a)
wait 700
snap 07_tract_back
tap f8
wait 1700
snap 08_tract_off
tap play
