# The TRACT page (docs/TRACT-KNOBS-PLAN.md A2, 3.3, 11.1; docs/PADS-TRACT-PLAN.md): Sample Playback opens TRACT (it used to open GROWL; again it flips to GROWL and back), F1 VOWEL,
# F2 TONGUE, F3 LIPS, F4 RES, F5 GLIDE, F6 SIZE, F7 TRC MIX, F8 the TRACT key (off is Mix 0, a bit-exact bypass; on gives the last mix back). There is one layer, the pattern's STEPS: the
# pads are the steps (rows 0 and 1, columns 0 to 7), the vowel keys A E I on row 2 and O U - on row 3 (they write the held steps, else the cursor step), TONGUE and LIPS up and down in
# columns 4 and 6, and the tools in columns 9 and 11 (tract_pads.txt). VOWEL reads a letter on a vowel's point ("A", "O") and "<nearest letter> <percent>" between ("E 37%").
#
#   mkdir -p <dir> && build-host/app/o3_303_sim --scenario app/scenarios/tract.txt --out <dir>
#
# 01 TRACT at a first press (F8 off); 02 F8 on (Mix 50 %); 03 the mouth narrowed and the mix up, the vowel O typed on step 0 by its key; 04 the mouth in between (F1 turned: "E 37%");
# 05 a dark pad (column 8) pressed: nothing changes; 06 Sample Playback again: GROWL; 07 again: TRACT back with its vowel; 08 F8 off. The WAV is the factory line: dry for the first bar,
# the tract on the pattern's vowels for two, dry again at the end (the bit-exact A/B of the bypass is tests/tract_page_test.cpp).
